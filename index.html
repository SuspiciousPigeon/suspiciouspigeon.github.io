The Game

A city pigeon leaves the streets behind and discovers a mysterious forest where its unusual skills might be exactly what the locals need.

Gather ingredients, grow medicinal crops, brew remedies, help forest creatures recover from illness, and uncover the truth behind a spreading plague.

Features

  • 🌿 Gather herbs and rare ingredients
  • 🚜 Build and expand a small farm
  • 🧪 Craft medicines and remedies
  • 🏪 Upgrade from a market stall to a full apothecary
  • 🗺️ Explore multiple forest regions
  • 🔍 Solve the mystery of a strange illness
